**Project description**

ClearLedger turns verified business invoices into programmable, financeable real-world assets. Small suppliers often wait weeks or months for payment, while lenders and on-chain liquidity providers lack a trusted way to verify the parties, the underlying obligation, and who may hold or transfer the financing position. ClearLedger creates a compliance-first invoice-financing flow: a verified supplier registers an invoice, the verified buyer confirms it, eligible liquidity providers fund it through a gated pool, and repayment settles with a complete on-chain audit trail. Cleanverse is essential to the design—not an add-on. CVI provides wallet-bound participant eligibility, CVA provides the verified settlement asset, and Cleanverse rule checks enforce transfer restrictions and compliant settlement before value moves. The hackathon demo will show issuance, funding, restricted transfer, repayment, and audit evidence end to end.

**Cleanverse integration plan**

During the hackathon, I will integrate CVI as the entry and transfer condition for suppliers, buyers, and liquidity providers; use CVA as the funding and repayment asset; and apply Cleanverse pre-transaction rules to reject ineligible transfers before settlement. The application will record only the minimum on-chain eligibility and asset references required for the demo, while personal information remains outside public contract state. I will expose the full flow in a simple web app and generate an audit-ready transaction history. After the hackathon, ClearLedger can add issuer onboarding, risk tiers, jurisdiction-specific policies, secondary-market transfers, and gateway-based fiat settlement for institutional pilots.

ClearLedger gives small suppliers faster access to working capital without asking investors to trust an opaque invoice token.
