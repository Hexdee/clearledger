# ClearLedger demo recording script

Target length: 3–4 minutes.

1. **The problem (20 seconds):** suppliers have real receivables but wait weeks for cash; financiers cannot safely underwrite anonymous wallets or unrestricted tokens.
2. **The product (25 seconds):** show the ClearLedger landing page and the issue → confirm → finance → settle lifecycle.
3. **Cleanverse at the core (35 seconds):** explain that CVI identifies all three participants, CVA is the financing and repayment asset, and CCP checks the pool policy before a transaction.
4. **Allowed path (60 seconds):** connect an A-Pass wallet, run the live preflight, show the approved decision, then finance the buyer-confirmed invoice on Monad.
5. **Blocked path (35 seconds):** switch to an ineligible wallet and show that the preflight rejects it before money moves.
6. **Settlement and evidence (45 seconds):** repay in the A-Token, claim as funder, and show the contract events and completed audit timeline.
7. **Close (20 seconds):** recap that compliance is embedded from issuance through repayment, not added after the asset exists.

Recording checklist: hide all secret values, show the network and deployed addresses, keep the browser zoom readable, and include repository/live-demo links in the video description.
